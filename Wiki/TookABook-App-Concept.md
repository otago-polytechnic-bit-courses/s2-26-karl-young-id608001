## App Concept

TookABook is a simple reading app that helps users store, read, and track progress across their ebooks and PDFs. It is designed for students and casual readers who want a home to organise their digital reading. Users can upload their own books/PDF's, open them in a clean, accessible reader, and see their progress over time. 
The core functionality of this app is that users can upload, read, and track their books and PDFs.

The single feature this app cannot exist without is the ability for users to upload their own books and PDFs.
One feature I'm tempted to add but will leave out is personalised book recommendations.

### Core Loop

A user uploads a book, reads it and sees their reading progress.

## 10 Requirements 

As a reader, I want to upload books so I can have all my reading in one place. - M

As a reader, I want to be able to open my books in the app so that I can read them easily. - M

As a reader, I want the app to remember my progress so that I can continue where I left off. - S

As a student, I want to be able to read PDFs both horizontally and vertically so that I can read different types of PDFs. - C

As a reader, I want to adjust the text size to make reading easier on my eyes. - S

As a reader, I want to be able to search through my books so I can find one when I have lots of them. - S

As a reader, I want to filter by genre to see which books I have in that genre. - C

As a student, I want to be able to bookmark a page so that I don't lose an important note. - S

As a reader, I want to be able to log in and see my uploaded books so that I know they are safe and in one place. - M

As a reader, I want to view my reading stats to see if I'm reading enough per month - W

### MoSCoW

The MoSCoW prioritisation technique was used to rank all requirements. Features were categorised as Must Have (M), Should Have (S), Could Have (C), or Won't Have (W) based on their importance to the delivery of a functional first release of the application.
